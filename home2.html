    Interactive Vehicle Diagnostic

    Is Your Car Experiencing Alignment Issues?

    Identify common tyre & steering symptoms before they cause costly suspension damage.

    Symptom: Vehicle Pulls to One Side

    If your car constantly drifts left or right when letting go of the steering wheel on a flat straight road, your Toe or Camber angle is misaligned.

    Recommended Solution: 3D Laser Toe Calibration

    Symptom: Steering Wheel Vibration

    Experiencing steering wheel shaking at 80-120 km/h indicates imbalanced wheel weight distribution or tire tread out-of-roundness.

    Recommended Solution: Computerized Dynamic Balancing

    Symptom: Uneven Tread Shoulder Wear

    If the inner or outer edge of your tyre tread is wearing down significantly faster than the center, your camber angles need urgent realignment.

    Recommended Solution: Full 4-Wheel Alignment & Rotation
