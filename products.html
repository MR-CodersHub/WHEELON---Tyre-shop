Showroom FAQ

Tyre Selection & Sizing Questions

How do I find the exact tyre size written on my current tyres?
↓
Look at the raised lettering on the outer sidewall of your tyre. You will see a sequence like **205/55 R16 91V**. Here 205 is tread width in mm, 55 is aspect ratio %, R16 is 16-inch rim diameter, 91 is load index, and V is speed rating.
Can I change my tyre width or profile without buying new rims?
↓
Yes, within a 3% overall diameter tolerance. Our showroom technicians can check your vehicle's fender clearance to recommend safe alternate sizes for wider grip or smoother ride cushion.
