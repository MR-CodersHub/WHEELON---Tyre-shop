Tyre Maintenance & Care FAQ

Where can I find the DOT manufacturing date code on my tyre?
↓
Look for a 4-digit number at the end of the DOT string on your tyre's outer sidewall (e.g. **2422**). The first two digits indicate the week of manufacture (Week 24) and the last two digits indicate the year (2022).
How should spare seasonal tyres be stored when not in use?
↓
Store tyres in a clean, cool, dark room away from direct sunlight, moisture, and electric motors (which emit rubber-degrading ozone). Tyres on rims should be hung or stacked horizontally; loose tyres should be stored standing upright.
