Our History

From Local Workshop to Premier Wheel Calibration Hub

Founded in 2011, ApexTyre & Wheel Alignment Center was built on a single core philosophy: **Every driver deserves formula-level steering precision and honest tyre advice without dealership price markups.**

What started as a 2-bay workshop has grown into a state-of-the-art 8-bay facility equipped with digital 3D camera alignment rigs, automatic touchless tyre dismounters, and high-purity nitrogen inflation stations.

01
