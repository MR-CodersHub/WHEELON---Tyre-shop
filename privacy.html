Legal & Security

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026 • ApexTyre & Wheel Alignment Center

1. Commitment to Privacy

At ApexTyre & Wheel Alignment Center ("ApexTyre", "WHEELON", "we", "us", or "our"), protecting your personal information and vehicle data is fundamental to how we operate. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, store, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, schedule service appointments, or utilize our 3D computerized wheel alignment and tyre services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to deliver precision automotive alignment, tyre fitting, and maintenance services:

  • Personal Contact Details: Full name, telephone number, email address, and billing address.
  • Vehicle Specifications: Vehicle make, model, year, VIN (Vehicle Identification Number), license plate number, mileage, and tire size parameters required for 3D computerized alignment database matching.
  • Service Records: Bay appointment bookings, diagnostic alignment reports, wheel balancing telemetry, replacement invoice history, and warranty records.
  • Technical Website Data: IP address, browser type, device identifiers, and language/RTL preferences stored locally for user experience.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is processed strictly for legitimate automotive service and operational purposes:

  • To schedule, confirm, and manage your bay service appointments.
  • To perform 3D laser alignment calibrations against OEM vehicle manufacturer specifications.
  • To send automated SMS/email appointment reminders and service completion notifications.
  • To issue tyre mileage warranties and service history receipts.
  • To improve website performance, dark/light theme persistence, and language direction preferences.

4. Data Protection & Security Controls

We implement enterprise-grade technical and organizational security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. All online booking communications are encrypted using standard TLS/SSL protocols. Access to vehicle diagnostic telemetry and customer accounts is strictly restricted to certified service technicians and authorized workshop personnel.

5. Third-Party Sharing & Disclosure

ApexTyre does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We share data only under the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Partners: Payment gateway processors for secure transaction authorization.
  • Tyre Manufacturers: Official warranty registration for purchased tyres (e.g., Michelin, Bridgestone, Continental).
  • Legal Requirements: When mandated by law, court order, or governmental regulatory authorities.

6. Cookies & Web Storage

We use essential local storage cookies (`apextyre_theme`, `apextyre_rtl`) to remember your preferred viewing modes (Dark/Light mode and RTL/LTR text direction). We do not utilize invasive tracking cookies or cross-site advertising scripts.

7. Your Rights & Choices

You have the right to request access to your service history records, request corrections to your contact information, or ask for the deletion of non-essential marketing data. To exercise these rights, please contact our privacy compliance team.
