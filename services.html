Frequently Asked Questions

Wheel Alignment & Service Knowledge FAQ

Get answers to common mechanical questions regarding wheel geometry and balancing.

What is the difference between 2-wheel and 4-wheel 3D alignment?
↓
2-wheel alignment only adjusts the front wheels relative to each other. 4-wheel 3D alignment uses laser targets on all four wheels to align the rear axle thrust angle with the vehicle centerline, ensuring your steering wheel stays perfectly straight on open highways.
How do I know if my wheels need balancing instead of alignment?
↓
If your vehicle pulls or drifts to the left or right, it needs **alignment**. If your steering wheel shakes or vibrates at specific speeds (e.g. 80-120 km/h), your wheels need **dynamic balancing**.
How long does a full 3D alignment service take?
↓
Our quad-camera 3D laser diagnostic scan takes less than 3 minutes. The entire measurement, adjustment of toe/camber tie-rods, and test drive typically takes 25 to 30 minutes in total.
