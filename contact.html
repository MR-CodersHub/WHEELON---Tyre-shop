Directions Guide

How to Reach ApexTyre Workshop

Follow simple Highway exit routes to enter our dedicated customer check-in parking area.

From North Highway Exit 12

Take Exit 12 south onto Auto Hub Parkway. Drive 400m straight; our service bay entrance is on your right side opposite the Shell station.

From South Ring Expressway

Head north along Commercial Boulevard, turn left onto Metro Parkway. Follow the red ApexTyre signage into the customer parking bay.

Customer Check-In Protocol

Park in designated slots #1-8. Our reception staff will perform a quick initial tread check before pulling your vehicle onto the hoist.
